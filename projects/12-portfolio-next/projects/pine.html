PPine Analytics

01Overview

Pine is a SaaS analytics product whose old UI rendered charts so slowly that customers exported to spreadsheets instead.

I rebuilt the dashboard layer: filters, cohorts, and custom dashboards that load in under a second and embed anywhere.

02Challenge

ClickHouse queries over billions of events had to feel instant, with dashboards that non-analysts could compose themselves.

03Solution

Pre-aggregated rollups on ClickHouse, a D3 chart layer with virtualized legends, and an embed API that ships the same dashboards into customer apps.
