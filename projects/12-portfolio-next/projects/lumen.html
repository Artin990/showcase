01Overview

Lumen needed a product-launch landing page that did more than look good — it had to collect a waitlist, measure every step, and survive a launch-day spike.

Two weeks from Figma to live, with animation, analytics, and a waitlist that fed their CRM.

02Challenge

Make it feel animated and premium without shipping a JS bundle that tanked Lighthouse on mid-range phones.

03Solution

Next.js static page with Framer Motion scoped to above-the-fold, waitlist writes through a rate-limited endpoint, and event analytics wired before launch day.
