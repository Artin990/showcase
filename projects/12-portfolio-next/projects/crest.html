CCrest App

01Overview

Crest is a habit-tracker mobile-web app with offline-first sync — log a run on the subway, and it reconciles when you surface.

Six weeks from prototype to app-store-quality PWA with gentle, non-guilt-tripping notifications.

02Challenge

Offline-first means treating the network as an optimization, not a requirement — conflicts from two devices editing the same week had to resolve themselves.

03Solution

Next.js PWA with IndexedDB as the local store, a last-write-wins merge with per-field timestamps, and a push notification scheduler the user fully controls.
