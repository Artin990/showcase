VVale Boutique

01Overview

Vale is a high-touch fashion label where every piece is made to order — their checkout had to capture measurements, timelines, and gift notes without feeling like a form.

A bespoke Shopify checkout flow with a Next.js storefront kept the brand experience intact end to end.

02Challenge

Shopify's standard checkout can't ask 'when do you need it?' or 'any sleeve adjustments?' without breaking the payment flow.

03Solution

A Next.js storefront with a custom cart that stages order notes per item, then hands a fully-formed draft order to Shopify's payment step — brand styling throughout.
