HHarbor Startup

01Overview

A maritime-tech startup needed a fund-me landing page with the pitch deck, a one-pager PDF, and an Airtable-backed investor list — before their demo day.

Three weeks later the page had raised the conversation (and the round) with clean analytics on who read what.

02Challenge

Investor pages live or die on trust — it had to read like a term sheet, load instantly, and track interest without creepy vibes.

03Solution

Next.js landing with a gated deck download (email in, PDF out), an Airtable pipeline for follow-ups, and motion kept subtle on purpose.
