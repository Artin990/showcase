01Overview

Birch sells small-batch soap and candles direct-to-consumer and wanted a Shopify custom theme with soul — plus a wholesale portal for their stockist network.

Four weeks: a custom Liquid theme, a gated wholesale portal, and a checkout the founders finally stopped apologizing for.

02Challenge

One Shopify store serving two very different buyers — retail customers browsing scents, and stockists placing 200-unit restock orders.

03Solution

A custom theme with a wholesale customer tag: stockists see tiered pricing, quick-order sheets, and order history, while retail keeps the editorial storefront.
