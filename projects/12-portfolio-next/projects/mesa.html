01Overview

Mesa Group runs four sub-brands off one marketing team, and their old setup was four WordPress installs drifting apart in design and content.

One multisite install with a shared Elementor design system now serves all four — the team publishes everywhere without a developer.

02Challenge

Four brands that must look related but not identical — and a marketing team that had never touched a staging environment.

03Solution

A WordPress multisite with a shared Elementor kit: brand tokens (color, type, spacing) drive per-site styling, while templates stay centrally maintained.
